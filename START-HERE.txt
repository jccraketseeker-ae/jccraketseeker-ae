READY-TO-PASTE WEBSITE FILES

1. Replace your current files with: index.html, blog.html, style.css, script.js, optin.css, and optin.js.
2. Keep your images folder and resume PDF in the same project folder.
3. In index.html, replace YOUR_FORM_ID with your real Formspree form ID.
4. Open index.html with Live Server and click Services to test the popup.
5. Upload/push all six files to GitHub.

You no longer need portfolio.html or index(1).html after index.html is working.
